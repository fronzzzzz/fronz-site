Custom price. Same weekly accountability. Same hands-on rule. Same $250/hr floor when scoping hours.

Spine:

1. Interpret the sprint data
2. Execute against it
3. Set up learning loops (product and messaging)
4. Iterate, ship, expand

No magic pills. If they will not stay in the work, you do not start. You are not their team.

## 2. Circuit breakers (before anyone is paid)

These are the practice rules. Merge will argue with every one of them. Keep them anyway.

**Free intro = 20 minutes.** Their completed GTM Clarity Map. You do not fill it in. If they skipped it, that is homework, not your hour.

**Unpaid cap = 45 minutes** to read what they sent, per live opportunity. Clock stops. Want more? That is week 1 of a paid sprint.

**No spec work.** No custom research, app audit, or proposal deck before kickoff. The call produces a yes/no on the container and a short read of what you heard (one paragraph, written in the call or immediately after). The workup is a sprint deliverable.

**One courtship at a time** until two sprints have run at this cap. Two parallel unpaid workups is how the pipeline dies.

**Hands-on is a gate.** Kickoff names their hours and decision rights. They own the call on position and tests. You do not make the call for them so the room feels finished.

## 2.5 Live-call circuit breakers (added Sep 3 2026 — Compass retro)

Pre-payment discipline (§2) is not the same as in-the-room discipline. Compass Sep 3 held every §2 rule *before* the call and violated §2.5 *during* the call. Both sets have to hold.

**The fork sentence is spoken, not planned.** If a call sheet contains a scripted read tied to a gate condition, the sentence is delivered **verbatim within 60 seconds of the gate opening**. Reflect-backs do not count as the read — agreement-shaped reflect-backs actively close the fork. After every qualifying question, ask internally: *did that answer open a scripted gate? If yes, deliver the line now — not later.*

**Proposal-shaped requests get container-shaped answers only.** When they say "shoot over what you're thinking / send me the scope / lay it out" — the only allowed live answer is:

> "The container is [name] · [price] · [your hours] · [their hours]. Custom scope is week 1 — that's part of what you're paying for. One-pager and invoice go out when we hang up."

**Forbidden live:** week-by-week deliverables, phase splits, deliverable lists, hypothesis frameworks. Those are §7 Tier C — post-deposit only. Narrating scope on a call = giving away week-1 sprint work for free.

**No downhill offers inside the same call.** If the primary container declines, the second offer waits for **re-initiation on a separate day.** Offering a smaller container as a same-call rescue is bending in a different shape — even when the primary price didn't move. Correct move at decline: warm close, "the container stands when you're ready," end the call.

*Exception:* the smaller container is offered mid-call only when it's the **pre-planned primary close for a different track** (e.g. $500 DLogos session in the Sep 3 sheet) — and only against that pre-planned track, not repurposed to rescue the declined one.

**Second-track pitch happens at the fork or not at all.** If the pivot to a second track didn't happen at the qualification fork, it does not happen at the end of the call either. End-of-call strategy on their real business is a giveaway with zero leverage. If a second track has to surface late, the only permitted shape is one sentence + invoice ask + silence. *"That's what I actually do — $500 session, this week. Want the invoice?"* Then stop.

**Never announce the sales move live.** Internal pattern-recognition ("I was testing you," "this is the paid version of the ping," "I'm holding the line here") stays internal. Naming your own choreography teaches a pattern-aware buyer to watch for it next time. Allowed: naming *their* pattern from their words. Forbidden: naming *your* pattern from your playbook.

**Count to ten after every ask. Twice.** Hands off keyboard, eyes off screen, physical count. If the silence isn't survivable at ten, the ask wasn't clean — rework it in a later call, do not rescue in this one. Rescued silence = the container never lands clean; buyer doesn't have to say no, softens into a "conversation" they keep thinking about. That is the warm maybe, the most expensive outcome.

**Merge budget applies live.** §5's caps count courtship prep. §2.5 adds: on a live call, if you catch yourself scoping deliverables, riffing on their real business, or filling silence with justification — that clock is running against Merge budget too. Name it in the recap.

### Live-call posture checklist (say out loud before the call)

- [ ] What is the fork condition + the exact fork sentence?
- [ ] What is the container ask + the price + the silence I have to survive after it?
- [ ] What is the *one* second-track ask allowed + when does it die?
- [ ] What am I forbidden from narrating live (scope, phases, deliverables, DLogos strategy)?
- [ ] What is the smallest paid close available today, and what does declining it look like without a rescue?

## 3. Waitlist (capacity is the feature)

When a sprint is active, or when two weeks of 15 hrs are already committed, you are not “quietly available.” You are on a waitlist.

What the waitlist is:

- Next sprint start date (a real date, even if it moves once)
- Required: GTM Clarity Map done
- Required: 20-minute intro booked into the **held weekly slot** (see §4)
- Message: “Next sprint starts [date]. Do the map. Book the intro.”

What the waitlist is not:

- A fake scarcity play while you still Merge into a third unpaid workup
- A content drip you have to write while delivering

If the intro calendar is empty because you cancelled it to stay in Compass, the waitlist is theater. Protect the slot.

## 4. What sells while you are in a sprint

There is no one else selling. So only assets that do not need live over-functioning get to be the funnel.

1. **GTM Clarity Map, always on.** Front door. Do not replace it with custom PDFs.
2. **One 20-minute intro hold per week, including during a sprint.** Miss it and the funnel is you again.
3. **Exit intros.** Every sprint exit review asks for two introductions. That is recirculation.

Do not, this quarter: a content engine, a second lead magnet, or a Groundswell campaign for Fronz. Those are more artifacts.

### Blocker — email capture is on the wrong stack

The homepage lead form (`/api/lead`) and the “send your map” form (`/api/starter`) both write to **Customer.io** using `CUSTOMERIO_SITE_ID` / `CUSTOMERIO_TRACK_API_KEY`. Delivery comment in the lead form: sending domain is not verified; people still get the map at `/map`. Capture can fail and they still pass through. That means:

- The list that should be the waitlist / map list may be empty, mixed with another workspace, or undeliverable.
- A waitlist you cannot email is a spreadsheet you will not open.

**Must migrate before the waitlist is real.** Either:

- A **dedicated Fronz Customer.io workspace** (not the current account), with a verified sending domain on fronzzz.com or a Fronz subdomain, **or**
- A **separate system** (list + transactional send) whose only job is map delivery, waitlist, and intro reminders.

Until that ships: the map still lives as a public page at `/map`. Intros still book on Calendly. Waitlist is a dated note plus people who booked, not an email campaign. Do not build the campaign on the current CIO account.

## 7. Courtship economics (Sep 2026)

**Lesson (JupiterBlock, Aug 2026):** JupiterBlock got a bespoke proposal page and ~10–18 unpaid hours before an instant stall. The diagnosis was right; the **courtship was the product** and it was priced at zero.

**Lesson (Compass, Sep 3 2026):** Same shape, different surface. No bespoke proposal page this time — but a live-call scope narration + free DLogos strategy read at end-of-call delivered the same product for free. ~8 unpaid hrs across the cycle; effective rate ≤$63/hr if $500 books later, $0/hr if nothing does. **Bespoke docs are not the only shape of "courtship as product" — live-call narration is the same failure mode in verbal form.** See `Client Meeting Notes/Compass Sep 3 - Post-Call Retro.md` + §2.5 above.

### The rule

> Custom scope is **sprint week 1**, not the preview. The preview is: GTM Clarity Map → 20-min Map Review → **container name + price** → invoice.

### Three proposal tiers — only these

| Tier | When | Your time cap | What they get |
|------|------|---------------|---------------|
| **A — Verbal + container** | End of 20-min intro | 15 min write-up | One paragraph read + link to **generic** site offer. Yes/no on container. |
| **B — One-pager** | Verbal yes, before deposit | **15 min** (template fill) | Bracketed PDF/email: container, price, hours, gate, next step. `{client}` fields only. |
| **C — Custom scope** | **After 50% deposit** | Sprint kickoff / week 1 | Deliverable list, custom copy, private URL if needed. Built **during** the sprint. |

**Never Tier C before payment.** No private Next.js proposal pages, no multi-iteration copy passes, no transcript re-reads beyond §2 unpaid cap.

### $250/hr floor (reference)

| Container | Price | Your hr cap | $/hr at cap |
|-----------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Check-in | $500 | 2 | $250 |
| Intensive | $2,500 | 10 | $250 |
| Sprint | **$7,500** | 30 | **$250** |
| On-call packs | $2,500 / 10 hr | 10 | $250 |

If courtship hours are unpaid, they **reduce effective rate**. Example: 15 hrs unpaid + 30 hrs sprint @ $7,500 = 45 hrs total → **$167/hr**. Keep courtship at Tier A/B only.

### What courtship is allowed to include

- Read their GTM Clarity Map (45 min cap)
- 20-min intro
- One paragraph: what you heard + recommended container
- Tier B one-pager from template (15 min)
- Invoice link

### What courtship is not allowed to include

- Custom research, competitor scans, app audits
- Bespoke proposal pages or decks
- Multi-day copy iteration pre-payment
- Free Telegram strategy threads
- “Let me think and send you a plan” (unpaid)
- **Live-call scope narration** (week 1 does X, week 2 does Y, handoff includes Z) — that is Tier C, post-deposit only. See §2.5.
- **End-of-call strategy on a second track they didn't buy** — no free DLogos read after Compass declined; no free positioning read after sprint declined. See §2.5.
